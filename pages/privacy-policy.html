Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains how I collect, use and protect your information.

📅 Last updated: May 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to codewithmpho.dev ("I", "me", "my"). I am committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information I collect, how I use it, and what rights you have in relation to it.

By visiting my website (codewithmpho.dev) and using my contact form or services, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

2. Information I Collect

I collect information that you voluntarily provide to me when you:

  • Fill out the contact form on my website
  • Send me an email directly
  • Engage with me on social media platforms
  • Hire me for freelance or contract work

The personal information I may collect includes:

  • Name – to address you appropriately
  • Email address – to respond to your enquiry
  • Phone number – if provided voluntarily
  • Project details / messages – to understand your needs

3. Automatically Collected Information

When you visit my website, certain information may be collected automatically, including:

  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent on pages
  • Referring URL
  • Device type and operating system
  • IP address (anonymised where possible)

This data is collected through standard server logs and analytics tools (if enabled) and is used solely to improve the performance and usability of the website.

4. How I Use Your Information

I use the information I collect to:

  • Respond to enquiries and contact form submissions
  • Provide freelance development and design services
  • Send project updates, invoices or relevant communications
  • Improve the content and functionality of my website
  • Comply with legal obligations

I do not sell, trade or rent your personal information to third parties.

5. Contact Form & Data Handling

When you submit the contact form on my website, the information you provide (name, email, subject and message) is sent directly to my email inbox. This data is:

  • Used only to respond to your enquiry
  • Stored only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose
  • Never shared with unauthorised third parties

6. Cookies

My website may use cookies to enhance your browsing experience. Cookies are small text files stored on your device. The types of cookies I may use include:

  • Strictly necessary cookies – required for the website to function
  • Analytics cookies – to understand how visitors use my site (anonymised)
  • Preference cookies – to remember your settings (e.g. theme preference)

You can control cookie settings through your browser. Disabling cookies may affect certain features of the website.

7. Third-Party Services

My website may use third-party services such as:

  • Google Fonts – for typography (subject to Google's Privacy Policy)
  • Netlify / Vercel – for hosting (subject to their respective privacy policies)
  • Email service providers – for processing contact form submissions

These services have their own privacy policies and I encourage you to review them.

8. Data Security

I take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and I cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • The right to access the information I hold about you
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate data
  • The right to request deletion of your data
  • The right to withdraw consent at any time
  • The right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

To exercise any of these rights, please contact me at officialcodewithmpho@gmail.com.

10. Children's Privacy

My website is not directed at children under the age of 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided me with personal data, please contact me and I will take steps to delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. I encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how I protect your information.
